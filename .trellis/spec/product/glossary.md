# Cue Player

A local YouTube player and material collection tool for learning in an external Agent. The vocabulary below describes the current product; planned learning-set and practice concepts are outside this glossary.

## Language

**Video（视频）**:
A saved YouTube learning source, shared by its reading subtitles, saved expressions even when it belongs to several collections.

**Subtitle Preference（字幕偏好）**:
The user's choice of manual-first or automatic English subtitles for a newly imported video. It is distinct from the subtitle source actually obtained.

**Subtitle Source（字幕来源）**:
The actual origin of a video's English subtitles: YouTube manual captions, YouTube automatic captions or local speech transcription.

**Raw Transcript（原始字幕）**:
The preserved textual and timing evidence obtained from a subtitle source, before later learning-oriented publication.
_Avoid_: Current reading subtitles, corrected draft

**Learning Transcript（学习字幕）**:
The timed English text used for reading and sentence replay, initially generated from Raw or later published after external processing and user confirmation.

**Transcript Revision（字幕版本）**:
One retained edition of a video's learning subtitles, with its own sentences and associated reading metadata.

**Current Revision（当前字幕版本）**:
The edition selected for the main reading list and subtitle overlay. Another retained edition may still provide the original context of older favorites.

**Segment（字幕条目）**:
One timed unit of learning subtitles. A preserved manual-caption unit need not be a complete linguistic sentence.

**Chunk Layout（词块布局）**:
A way of grouping the English text within a subtitle edition into natural reading chunks, without replacing that text or defining separate playback times.
_Avoid_: A separate subtitle version, learning card

**Translation（中文译文）**:
The optional saved Chinese rendering of a learning-subtitle entry, shared by its sentence and chunk views.

**Selection（所选表达）**:
A continuous span of English text within one subtitle entry and edition, or the whole entry. Equal words at different positions are distinct selections.

**Favorite（收藏）**:
A saved English selection with its original subtitle-entry context, exportable for learning in an external Agent. “字幕标记” is the older name for this same saved-expression concept.
_Avoid_: Automatically generated learning item

**Collection（视频合集）**:
A persistent ordered grouping of videos for viewing. Removing a member or the collection leaves those videos and their learning material intact.
_Avoid_: Import batch, chapter, Study Set

**Import Preview（导入预览）**:
A review of candidate inputs and ordering before the user confirms a video import or batch submission.

**Import Batch（导入批次）**:
The record of one batch submission and its per-item processing outcomes, independent of a persistent video collection.

**Import Job（视频处理任务）**:
One processing attempt to prepare a video's subtitles. A newly accepted retry has a new attempt while the previous failure remains recorded.
_Avoid_: Trellis development task

**Ready（字幕就绪）**:
The state indicating a video has reading subtitles available; it says nothing about whether the user watched or mastered the material.
